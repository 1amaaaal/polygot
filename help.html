How to play

Name the programming language in 6 guesses. A new snippet arrives every day at midnight, your local time.

Clues

You start with 3 lines of code. Every wrong guess reveals 2 more lines and compares your guess with the answer:

Java Object-oriented 1995 ↑ Static VM C-like
  • Same as the answer
  • Year within 5 years of the answer
  • Different from the answer
Paradigm
Its main style: object-oriented, functional, procedural, multi-paradigm, logic or declarative.
Year
When it first appeared. The arrow points towards the answer: ↑ means the answer is newer.
Typing
Static (types checked before running), dynamic (checked while running) or untyped.
Runs as
How it usually runs: compiled to native code, on a virtual machine, interpreted, JIT-compiled or transpiled to another language.
Looks like
The syntax family it resembles, such as C-like, Lisp or ML-like.

Hard mode 🔥

Turn it on in Settings (the gear, top right) before your first guess of the day. Syntax colouring is switched off, and every guess has to be a language that could still be the answer given all the clues so far. Hard-mode results get a *, like 3/6*.

Challenges ⚔️

After any round, press Challenge a friend to copy a link to the same snippet, with your score attached. Your friend sees your score and has to beat it. Add your GitHub username in the sidebar so they know who's challenging them.

Collection 🧩

Every language you solve, in any mode, is added to your collection. Can you collect all 35?

Leaderboard

Daily wins earn points: 6 for solving in one guess, down to 1 for six guesses. The leaderboard counts the last 7 days, so a new player can top it in their first week. Polyglot has no server, so there's no global leaderboard yet: you compete against a field of bots that play a simulated game every day.

Install it 📱

Polyglot works offline and can be installed like an app. On a phone, open your browser's menu and choose Add to Home screen (Android) or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone). On a computer, look for the install icon in the address bar.

Privacy

Everything (your stats, streak, collection and username) is stored only in your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no server.
